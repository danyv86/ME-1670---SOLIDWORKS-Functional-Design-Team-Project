# ME-1670---SOLIDWORKS-Functional-Design-Team-Project

Each team should have 2-3 students (Capstone projects) and 5
students (SDG Projects) from the same section and work on ideation,
sketching, planning, designing, modeling, assembly, check for
functionality and documentation of proposed 3D visualization project.
Each student is responsible for

o Modeling minimum of 10 parts / student (simple prismatic blocks, nuts and
bolts are not considered as parts. Each part must involve some level of
geometric complexity).

o The tasks must be distributed among the group members such that each
member can create at least a sub-assembly from his allocated/chosen
parts

o Contribute to at least one sub-assembly and renderings for the parts and

o Contribute to team efforts such as planning, preparing minutes of the
meeting, final assembly modeling, creating animation and motion
mechanisms, preparing assembly instruction manual, documentation etc.
